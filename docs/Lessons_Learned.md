# Lessons Learned  

## Milla Karjalainen  
I learned a lot about how to approach this kind of project and how the overall process works. At times it was challenging to understand the purpose of some tasks at first, but in the end it came together as a clear overall solution. In the future, I could improve by understanding the goals of each phase earlier and connecting them more clearly to the final system.  

## Sara Virtanen  
It was surprisingly challenging to adapt to the mindset and way of working where the vision of the future determines what gets built. Ultimately, I felt like it was a great example of illustrating how design should happen. All the steps in the process were laid out clearly, but it wasn't always easy to produce the expected results, and I feel like we consistently failed to deliver what we were supposed to. In this sense, working on this project was frustrating at times, but many lessons were learned and I would say I gained a new way of approaching projects.
